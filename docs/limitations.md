# Limitations

- The prompt set, while broad, cannot capture the full cultural nuance of every genre.
- Automatic metrics (e.g., MFCC similarity) are imperfect proxies for human perception.
- The benchmark is prompt-to-audio focused; it does not evaluate multimodal conditioning or lyrics quality beyond simple vocal handling scores.
- Rater bias can affect results; mitigation: multiple raters, training, and calibration sessions.
