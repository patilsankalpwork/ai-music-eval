# Dataset Design & Rationale

## Balance and coverage
- 150 genres × 3 prompts each = 450 prompts to ensure statistical power for per-genre evaluation.
- Genres are non-overlapping by design; duplicates are not permitted.

## Prompt diversity
- Each genre includes prompts that vary in tempo, mood, and structural emphasis to test model adaptability.

## Cultural representation
- Aim to include global genres; prompts include suggested instruments and respectful stylistic cues.
- See `rater_instructions.md` for guidelines on cultural-sensitivity evaluation.

## Ethical considerations
- Avoid exoticizing or appropriating cultural elements.
- Prompts that reference specific sacred traditions were vetted and made generic where appropriate.
